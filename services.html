  • Cash Flow Management: Analysis of your cash flow patterns and development of strategies to improve working capital management, ensure you have adequate cash for operations, and optimize the timing of income and expenses.
  • QuickBooks Setup & Training: Professional setup and customization of QuickBooks for your specific business needs, plus one-on-one training for you and your staff to ensure you can effectively manage your day-to-day bookkeeping.
  • Business Performance Analysis: Regular review of key performance indicators, profitability analysis, and recommendations for improving operational efficiency and profitability.

Specialized Tax Services

We offer specialized services for clients with unique tax situations or those facing specific tax challenges. Our expertise extends beyond routine tax preparation to include complex tax matters that require specialized knowledge and experience.

  • IRS & State Tax Audit Representation: If you're selected for an audit by the IRS or Pennsylvania Department of Revenue, we provide full representation, handle all communications with tax authorities, gather necessary documentation, and work to achieve the best possible outcome.
  • Tax Resolution Services: Assistance for clients with back taxes, tax liens, or other tax problems. We help negotiate payment plans, offer in compromise settlements, penalty abatements, and other resolution options with tax authorities.
  • Retirement Planning Guidance: Strategic advice on retirement account contributions, Traditional IRA vs. Roth IRA decisions, 401(k) optimization, and required minimum distribution planning to help you build retirement savings tax-efficiently.
  • Estate & Trust Tax Preparation: Preparation of estate tax returns (Form 706) and fiduciary income tax returns (Form 1041) for estates and trusts, helping executors and trustees navigate complex tax requirements during difficult times.
  • Non-Profit Organization Accounting: Specialized accounting and tax services for 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations including Form 990 preparation, ensuring compliance with non-profit tax regulations, and maintaining proper financial records for board reporting and grant applications.
  • Sales Tax Preparation: Preparation and filing of Pennsylvania Sales & Use Tax returns for businesses, ensuring proper collection, reporting, and payment of sales taxes to maintain compliance with state requirements.
  • Multi-State Tax Compliance: For businesses operating across state lines, we handle multi-state tax registration, apportionment of income, and compliance with various state tax requirements.

Pennsylvania-Specific Tax Expertise

As a Scranton-based firm, we have deep expertise in Pennsylvania-specific tax laws and regulations that out-of-state firms may miss. We stay current with Pennsylvania Department of Revenue updates, understand local tax requirements like Local Earned Income Tax (EIT) and Local Services Tax (LST), and are familiar with Pennsylvania-specific credits and deductions that can benefit our clients.

Our Pennsylvania expertise includes understanding the unique aspects of Pennsylvania's tax system such as the lack of a standard deduction, the treatment of retirement income, property tax rebate programs, and various tax credits available to Pennsylvania residents and businesses. This local knowledge can translate into significant tax savings for our clients.
